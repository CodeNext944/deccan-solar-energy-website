# deccan-solar-energy-website
Official website for Deccan Solar Energy featuring rooftop solar solutions, savings calculator, service pages, consultation flow, and customer trust-building sections.

## Project Overview
This project contains the website structure and content plan for Deccan Solar Energy.

The website focuses on:
- Rooftop solar solutions
- Electricity bill savings
- Solar consultation
- Site visit booking
- Subsidy assistance
- Net metering support
- Solar AMC and maintenance services
- Customer trust-building sections

## Brand Positioning
**Making solar simple, accessible, and powerful for every home and business.**
Deccan Solar Energy aims to help homeowners and businesses reduce electricity costs by adopting reliable, professionally installed rooftop solar systems.

## Solar Savings Calculator
The website should include a solar savings calculator to help customers understand their potential savings.

### Inputs
- Monthly electricity bill
- City or pincode
- Property type
- Available rooftop area

### Outputs
- Recommended solar system size
- Estimated monthly savings
- Estimated yearly savings
- Payback period
- Subsidy estimate

This section helps improve lead generation by giving users instant financial clarity.

## Process Flow=
The website should explain the solar installation journey in a simple 4-step process.

### Suggested Process
1. **Free Rooftop Survey**  
   Our team visits your location and checks solar installation feasibility.

2. **Custom Solar Design**  
   A solar system is designed based on your electricity usage and rooftop space.

3. **Quick Installation & Subsidy Support**  
   The system is installed professionally with guidance for subsidy and net metering.

4. **Enjoy Green Energy**  
   Start saving on electricity bills with ongoing professional maintenance support.
